Preview template. Replace the business details and review this policy before launch.

Important: This website is a static storefront preview. It does not currently accept orders, take payments, run account registration, or send newsletter subscriptions. This page must be updated if those services, analytics tools, advertising pixels, or third-party checkout providers are added.

1. Who operates this store

This storefront is presented as भद्रा कलेक्शन. Before launch, the business owner should add the correct legal business name, postal address, customer-support contact details and applicable jurisdiction. Do not publish this template as final legal advice.

2. Information handled by this preview

The preview does not ask you to provide personal details. Items added to the shopping bag are stored in your browser's local storage so the bag can remain available in that browser. The preview does not send this bag to a store server. Clearing site data or using another browser may remove the saved bag.

3. Payments and orders

Checkout is disabled. No payment card or bank details should be entered into this preview, and no order is placed by adding items to the bag. A production store must explain what data its payment gateway, shipping provider and order-management system collect and how those providers use it.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

This preview does not intentionally load analytics or advertising scripts. The externally hosted fonts and product photographs may cause your browser to contact their respective providers to retrieve those assets. Before launch, list all cookies, analytics, marketing tools and third-party assets actually used by the live store, and provide any required consent controls.

5. Sharing and retention

This preview has no customer database and does not upload the bag to a server. If the live store later collects order, account, support or marketing data, explain the purpose, lawful basis where applicable, recipients, retention period and deletion process here.

6. Your choices and requests

For the static preview, you can remove bag items in the bag panel or clear this site's browser storage. Before launch, add a monitored customer-support contact and clear instructions for privacy, access, correction, deletion and complaint requests as applicable under law.

7. Children's privacy

This store is intended for ordinary retail browsing and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not add account, marketing or tracking features without considering age-appropriate notices and applicable legal requirements.

8. Changes to this policy

Update this page whenever the live store's practices change. Add an accurate effective date after the business owner has reviewed and approved the final policy.
